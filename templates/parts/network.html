Test closing or opening a warehouse A what-if; your results stay as they are

Close a site and its routes move to the nearest site with room. Name a city to try a new site, and the routes closer to it move across. Every affected route is repriced.

Without one, it is assumed to take up to 50 percent more than it handles today, and each site is labelled so you can tell a stated limit from an assumed one. A new site is priced at the median cost and energy per tonne of your existing sites, on an average grid.
