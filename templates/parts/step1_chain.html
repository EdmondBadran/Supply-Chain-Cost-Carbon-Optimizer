The band gets thicker wherever cost is added, so you are watching cost build up on your goods as they move. Suppliers and customers leave it flat, because their cost belongs to the freight leg on either side of them and is counted there once. The line running backwards underneath is returns, travelling the wrong way and paying for the trip a second time. Pick any stage to open it.
