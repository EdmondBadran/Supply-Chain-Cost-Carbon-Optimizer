The band thickens wherever cost is added, so you are looking at money accumulating on your goods as they move. Suppliers and customers leave it flat because their cost belongs to the leg either side of them, counted once. The leg running backwards underneath is returns, going the wrong way and paying for the trip a second time. Pick any stage to open it.
