Which assumptions affect the estimates?

  • Cost rates are typical industry rates per tonne-km, not your contract prices.
  • Emission rates follow DEFRA and GLEC, the basis of most freight carbon reporting.
  • Distances are straight lines with an allowance for each transport mode, not real road, rail or sea routes.
  • Transit times are planning estimates, not carrier schedules.
  • A change is listed only if it cuts both cost and CO₂e by at least {{ pct(flag_threshold) }}.
    • {% if report and report.problems|selectattr("kind", "equalto", "supplier_on_time")|list %}
  • Late-delivery savings assume {{ pct(expedite_share) }} of late deliveries are flown in.
    • {% endif %} {% set cover = ingest.coverage if ingest else None %} {% if cover and cover.basis == "scaled" %}
  • The figures are a year scaled from {{ cover.span_days }} days of orders, so a busy or quiet period carries into the annual total.

  • The figures are the year in the file, and assume next year ships like it.
