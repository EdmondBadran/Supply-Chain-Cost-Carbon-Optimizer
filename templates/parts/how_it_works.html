1. What it works out

A yearly cost and CO₂e estimate for every route

You upload a year of shipping orders. Orders that travel between the same two places by the same transport mode are grouped into one route, so thousands of order lines become a few dozen routes.

For each route, the weight shipped times the distance times a standard rate for its transport mode gives a yearly freight cost and CO₂e. Warehouse, packaging and returns are added on top. Emission rates follow DEFRA and GLEC, the basis of most freight carbon reporting. Cost rates are typical industry rates, not your contract prices.

2. What it recommends

Only changes that cut cost and CO₂e together

Each route is priced again on every realistic alternative, such as sea instead of air on a long route, or rail instead of road. A change is listed only if it cuts both the route's cost and its CO₂e by at least {{ pct(flag_threshold) }}. The list is ranked by combined impact.

Every route change is then re-run {{ confidence_checks }} times with the cost and emission rates varied. High confidence means it still qualified in at least {{ pct(confidence_high) }} of those re-runs. The figures are planning estimates for deciding where to look, not quotes.
