What if the network were shaped differently

Close a site and its work moves to whichever remaining site is nearest and has room, and you stop paying for the building. Open a new one and the routes that are closer to it move across. Every affected route is reassigned and repriced from scratch, not estimated.

A site that states a capacity in your warehouse file is held to it. Without one, it is assumed to take up to 50 percent more than it handles today, and each site is labelled so you can tell a measured limit from an assumed one. Anything that will not fit goes to the next nearest site, and the result says so. A new site is priced at the median cost and energy per tonne of your existing sites, on an average grid, because nothing in your data describes a building that does not exist yet.
