What if your warehouses were somewhere else

Untick a warehouse to close it. Its work moves to whichever of your remaining buildings is nearest and has space, and you stop paying rent and power on the one you closed. Name a city to try opening a new one. Every affected route is worked out again from scratch, not guessed.

If your warehouse file says how much a building can hold, that limit is used. If it does not, each building is assumed to cope with half as much again as it handles today, and every row says which of the two it used. Anything that will not fit goes to the next nearest building, and the answer tells you when that happens. A new building is costed at what your existing ones cost per tonne, because nothing in your data describes a place that does not exist yet.
