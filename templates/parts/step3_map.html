How capacity and new sites are handled

A site that states a capacity in your warehouse file is held to it. Without one, it is assumed to take up to 50 percent more than it handles today, and each site is labelled so you can tell a measured limit from an assumed one. Anything that will not fit goes to the next nearest site, and the result says so. A new site is priced at the median cost and energy per tonne of your existing sites, on an average grid.
