Or load your own

A CSV export of order lines. Anything the loader cannot read is reported back by line number and the rest of the file still loads, so one bad row does not cost you the import. Nothing you load is stored.

Which columns each file needs

Orders, required: origin_name, origin_city, dest_city, weight_kg, mode. Optional: order_ref, order_date, customer_id, origin_country, dest_country, units, product_category, order_value, returned. Add origin_lat / origin_lon / dest_lat / dest_lon for anywhere too small for the city lookup.

Warehouses, optional: name plus storage_cost_annual, energy_kwh_annual, grid_intensity and capacity_kg. Give a site a capacity and closing its neighbour is checked against what it can really hold; leave it out and a headroom assumption stands in, which the answer says out loud.

Suppliers, optional: name, city, supplies (the warehouse it feeds), mode and annual_weight_kg, plus country, shipments_per_year, annual_cost, lead_time_days, min_order_qty and on_time_rate. Add it and the chain runs end to end instead of starting at your warehouses.

Transport modes are road, rail, sea and air. Aliases like truck, ground, ship and plane are read as the right mode. City names can be spelled properly: Malmö and Jönköping resolve the same as Malmoe and Joenkoeping.
