Optional order columns

order_ref (or order_id), order_date, customer_id, origin_country, dest_country, units, product_category, order_value and returned. A country makes each city unambiguous. For a place too small for the city lookup, add origin_lat, origin_lon, dest_lat and dest_lon.

Truck, LTL, ocean freight and air cargo are read as road, road, sea and air. Accented city names work either way: Malmö and Malmoe are the same place.

Files saved from Excel work whether they use commas or semicolons, and in a semicolon file 52,46 is read as 52.46.

What happens next

  1. We check every row and tell you anything we could not read.
  2. Orders on the same route are grouped, and each route is priced on realistic alternatives.
  3. You get a ranked list of changes, usually within a few seconds.

What if a column is missing?

When you choose your file we check its columns, and ask which of yours holds anything we cannot find, before it is sent. Rows we cannot read are listed by line number, and the rest of the file still loads.
