Terms of use

Overlap screens shipping data for routes where cost and carbon could both come down. What it produces is an estimate to investigate, and these terms are mostly about what that does and does not mean.

{{ icon("info") }} Draft. This is a practical product draft written by the person who built the tool, not by a lawyer, and it has not been reviewed by one. It describes how Overlap actually behaves today. Before this tool is sold or relied on commercially, this page and the privacy page need a legal review.

What the results are

Planning estimates. Every figure is modelled from your order data using published emission factors, default cost factors and typical transit speeds, all of which the tool prints under Data and assumptions. They are produced to tell you which routes are worth a closer look and in what order.

What the results are not

  • Not a carrier quote. No carrier has priced anything here. Rates, surcharges, capacity, lane contracts and border constraints are not modelled.
  • Not financial or investment advice. Nothing on these pages is a recommendation about spending, budgeting or capital allocation, and Overlap is not a regulated adviser.
  • Not a guaranteed saving. An estimated annual saving is what the model produces under its own assumptions. It is not a promise, a forecast, or a number to put in a budget or a report to a board without checking it.
  • Not operational instructions. A change marked Ready to act is ready to be reviewed, not ready to be executed. Do not rebook freight, change a mode or close a site on the strength of this tool alone.
  • Not an audit, certification or regulatory filing. The emissions figures are screening estimates and are not prepared to any reporting standard.

Your data and your responsibility

You are responsible for having the right to upload whatever you upload, and for the decisions you take afterwards. Do not upload anything you are not allowed to share with a third party service. What happens to a file once it arrives is set out on the privacy page.

Availability

Overlap is offered as-is, with no guarantee of availability, and it is hosted in a way that can end your session without warning. Your analysis lives in memory only. Export what you need to keep.

Anyone acting on a figure from this tool is doing so on their own commercial judgement, having checked it with the people who run the lane.
