What each mode actually costs you

The freight rate is what a tonne-kilometre costs and emits in transit. The all-in figure adds packaging, the warehouse and the return leg, which is what serving the route actually takes. The gap between the two columns is the part of cost to serve that a freight quote never shows.

Expect the gap to be enormous on short domestic routes and almost nothing on long sea ones. Packaging and warehousing do not care how far a parcel travels, so on a twenty kilometre run there are barely any tonne-kilometres to divide them by. That is not a pricing error, it is the reason cost per tonne-kilometre is the wrong measure for short haul and the right one for comparing modes.
