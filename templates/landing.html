How it works

  1. 1

    Upload your orders

    A CSV of completed shipments: where each one started, where it ended, its weight and how it travelled.

  2. 2

    We find viable route changes

    Each route is priced on realistic alternatives, like sea instead of air on a long route. Only changes that cut both cost and CO₂e by at least {{ pct(flag_threshold) }} are kept.

  3. 3

    Review savings and export your plan

    See what each change could save a year, how sure to be and what to check, then share a summary or a workbook.

Why you can check the numbers

  • Published emission factors

    CO₂e is estimated with DEFRA and GLEC factors per tonne-kilometre, the basis of most freight carbon reporting.

  • Route changes are stress-tested

    Each one is re-run {{ confidence_checks }} times with different cost and emission rates, and says how often it held up.

  • Calculations you can open

    Every change shows its arithmetic, and every assumption is listed beside the results.

  • Nothing stored
