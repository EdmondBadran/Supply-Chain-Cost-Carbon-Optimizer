How this is different from what already exists

Most tools show what freight costs or what it emits, not both. Getting both normally means a network modelling project. This screens a spreadsheet you already have and ranks the routes worth a closer look.

Carbon reporting tools

Show what a route emits, not what it costs, so they cannot say which is worth fixing first.

Freight spend analytics

Show what a route costs from real invoices, and leave carbon out.

Network design software

Does both with an optimisation solver, but needs the data, setup and expertise of a full modelling project.

This

Screens a CSV export and ranks routes where cost and carbon can both be cut, in about a second.

Why you can check it

  • Published factors Emissions per tonne-km come from the DEFRA and GLEC ranges, the basis of most freight carbon reporting.
  • Confidence on every change Every factor is redrawn thousands of times, and each recommendation says how often it still held.
  • Every calculation shown Open any route to see the arithmetic behind it. The report lists every factor with its source, and the full method is available on request.
  • Nothing lost, nothing kept Every row is either processed or listed with the reason it was not, and the file is dropped after the analysis. What happens to your file.
